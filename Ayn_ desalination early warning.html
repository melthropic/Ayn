    Data sources

    • Chlorophyll — real. Snapshot from the NOAA NESDIS DINEOF gap-filled ocean-colour product (NOAA-20/Suomi-NPP VIIRS + Copernicus Sentinel-3A OLCI, ~9 km, daily) via the open CoastWatch ERDDAP. Production equivalent: Copernicus Marine GLOBAL_ANALYSISFORECAST_BGC_001_028 (0.25°, daily, ~12:00 UTC) or the satellite L4 NRT ocean-colour record.
    • Currents + SST — real. HYCOM/NCODA GLBy0.08 surface analysis, via open OPeNDAP. Production equivalent: Copernicus Marine GLOBAL_ANALYSISFORECAST_PHY_001_024 (1/12°, hourly surface currents, updated daily).
    • Advection. The chlorophyll field is transported with a semi-Lagrangian backtrace along the current snapshot. A live build would step through the hourly forecast current fields instead.
    • Plant data — simulated. No real plant telemetry exists here. Training data for the cleaning model is generated from a simplified fouling model (pressure drop rising with feed organic load, temperature and time since the last clean) and trained in your browser.
    Chlorophyll, currents and SST in this page are a real, dated snapshot (see Data sources). All plant telemetry, capacities and the fouling model behind the cleaning forecast are simulated in this page — a live version would read each plant’s control system and the Copernicus Marine hourly feeds. Output at risk is a rough estimate, not a measured loss. The forecast band is a quantile model output, not a guarantee.
